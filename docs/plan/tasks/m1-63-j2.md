# M1-63 — The J2 term

Phase: E | Status: not started
Prerequisites: M1-07, M1-62

## Purpose

`realism.md` calls J2 *"the largest single accuracy gain per line of code in the
whole document"*. Earth's oblateness dominates every other perturbation by about
three orders of magnitude: it regresses an ISS-like orbit's node by 5° per day,
it is why sun-synchronous orbits exist, and it is what phase F is going to draw.

## What to implement

- **`ZonalGravity`**, a new `ForceTerm` alternative, carrying J2 and — since the
  structure costs nothing — the ability to hold J3 and J4 later, unused and
  zero in this milestone.
- **The symmetry axis is the true pole, not the ICRF z-axis.** The J2 field is
  aligned with Earth's figure axis, and at a 2026 epoch that differs from the
  J2000 pole by about 0.36° of precession. So the term rotates the state into
  the equator of date using M1-07, evaluates, and rotates back. Skipping that
  would be a 0.36° misalignment of the dominant perturbation — a model error
  large enough to show in phase F's precession budget, which is why it is done
  properly here.
- **The constants, consistent with each other**: J2, the reference radius and
  `mu` must come from **one** gravity model, since J2's effect scales with
  `(Re/r)²` and mixing an EGM96 J2 with a different Re is a silent few-parts-in-
  ten-thousand error. The chosen set is named, cited, and used everywhere.
- Dependency trait `Position`, so a future symplectic integrator accepts it.

## Out of scope

J3, J4 and the full spherical-harmonic field. Any other body's oblateness.
Tesseral terms and the resonance effects they cause.

## Tests

`tests/test_j2.cpp`.

- **Analytic values at the two easy places**: on the equator and over the pole,
  the J2 acceleration has closed forms, worked out in the test by hand.
- **The magic latitude**, which is the singularity worth testing here: the
  radial component of the J2 acceleration vanishes where `sin²φ = 1/5`, at
  φ = 26.565°. A sign error or a transposed term moves it, and nothing else in
  the suite would notice.
- **Symmetry**: reflecting the position through the equator flips the
  z-component and leaves the others unchanged, exactly.
- **Magnitude**: at 400 km altitude the J2 acceleration is about 1e-3 of the
  point-mass acceleration — the ratio that justifies including it and excluding
  J3, checked against a hand-computed value.
- **The pole is the pole of date**: the term's symmetry axis agrees with
  M1-07's precession to 1e-12, and a test with precession forced to identity
  produces a measurably different acceleration — so the rotation is proved to be
  doing something rather than being decorative.
- **Every scale**: the term is exercised at the Moon, Earth, Jupiter and Sun's
  radii, with J2 values appropriate to each, because a hidden Earth-sized
  constant is this project's already-shipped defect.

## Verification

The standing rules. The secular rates that J2 produces are checked once there is
an integrator to produce them — M1-66 for consistency and M1-77 for the budget.
